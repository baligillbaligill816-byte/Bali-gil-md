Add these lines into your existing menu command where other commands are listed (do not overwrite your whole menu if it already exists).

// --- Insert into your menu text ---
menuText += '\n📚 Misc\n';
menuText += '• .welcome - Send the Akatsuki welcome message\n';
menuText += '• .goodbye - Send the Exile notice goodbye message\n\n';

menuText += '🎵 Music\n';
menuText += '• .song <title> - Search and return a link for a song\n\n';
// --- End insert ---

If your menu is dynamically generated from the commands folder, no edit is needed: after you restart the bot the new commands/welcome.js, commands/song.js and commands/goodbye.js will be picked up automatically (provided your loader reads command.name and command.description).
